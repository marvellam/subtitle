# Review Policy

## Auto Replace

Use `auto` only when the wrong form has no reasonable valid reading in this course context.

Examples:
- `圣代` -> `宋代` in Song painting lessons.
- `钢笔花鸟画` -> `工笔花鸟画`.
- `素墨` -> `宿墨` in ink-use context.

## Conditional Replace

Use `conditional` when the wrong form may be valid elsewhere. The `condition` field should list nearby trigger tokens.

Examples:
- `硬笔` -> `用笔` only near `骨法|线条|勾线|谢赫`.
- `发托` -> `花托` only near `芙蓉|花|植物|花蕊`.

## Review Only

Use `review` for plausible terms that require listening or subject expertise.

Examples:
- Unclear material names.
- Pronoun changes when antecedent is ambiguous.
- `的/地/得` when grammar depends on sentence reconstruction.

## Speech Fillers

Remove standalone filler blocks and sentence-final fillers when deletion does not damage meaning. Mark discourse words for review rather than deleting aggressively.
